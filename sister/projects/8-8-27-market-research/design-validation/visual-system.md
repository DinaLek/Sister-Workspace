# Editorial Momentum — Visual System v0.1

Status: design-validation candidate. MOTIV8 is a codename, not a launch name.

## Creative idea

Personal growth is shown as continuity inside ordinary life. The system should feel like an independent lifestyle magazine meeting a calm, useful planning system: editorial rather than aspirational, precise rather than rigid, human rather than therapeutic.

## Palette

- Warm Ivory `#F4F0E7`: primary ground.
- Paper `#FFFCF6`: raised cards and text fields.
- Ink `#171717`: primary type.
- Electric Cobalt `#2457F5`: action, target and forward movement.
- Warm Coral `#F06B57`: interruption, adaptation and human warmth.
- Soft Grey `#C9C6BE`: rules, inactive states and metadata.
- Muted Ink `#686660`: secondary copy.

Use one accent per post. Cobalt is the default action color; coral is reserved for the changed plan or emotional interruption. Do not split a composition 50/50 between both accents.

## Typography

Instrument Sans is the display face. Inter is used for labels, metadata and small explanatory copy. Use 500, 600 and 700 only. Hooks should be manually line-broken and understood within one second. Never use small copy to carry the main idea.

## Photography

Show observed moments, not models: people from the side or behind, partial crops, mid-action gestures, natural light and ordinary contemporary environments. Direct eye contact, staged laughter, luxury fantasy, glossy skin, dream haze and text generated inside the image are disallowed.

Use a subtle grain overlay to unify generated and non-generated media. Keep negative space for copy, but do not make every subject occupy the same third of the frame. Include object metaphors and people-free frames to keep the feed varied.

## Composition grammar

- 72px minimum safe margin on 1080px-wide assets.
- One large proposition per frame.
- Editorial metadata appears in Inter uppercase at small size: series, issue, time or capacity.
- Thin rules, route lines, brackets and small circles create the system layer.
- Underlines and scale shifts replace decorative font mixing.
- Photography may bleed; text cards should feel placed, not centered by default.

## Template families

1. Hero Quote: one line, editorial crop or type-only ground.
2. Editorial Carousel: cover, 3–5 useful beats, one closing action.
3. Scenario A/B: rigid plan versus adaptive next step.
4. System Diagram: capacity, minimum, progress path or rerouting.
5. Meme/Recognition: one familiar situation and a short reframing.
6. Faceless Reel: 2–4 text beats over type, photography or a restrained AI scene.

## Motion

Use slide, mask reveal, reframing, route drawing and calm scale changes. A plan changing should visibly reroute rather than disappear. Use smooth `power3.out` or equivalent easing. No bounce, elastic motion, spin transitions or high-speed motivation edits. Reel duration is 7–15 seconds and the hook is present at frame one.

## Do / Don't

Do: ordinary Tuesday, visible imperfection, quiet motion, one accent, generous negative space, meaningful system marks, diverse adults 27–45.

Don't: beige wellness sameness, hustle language, direct-to-camera AI portraits, decorative gradients, fake app UI, overloaded iconography, three accents in one post, copied competitor layouts.

## Image provenance

Every generated asset records: prompt, provider, model if returned, seed if returned, generation date, usage notes and consuming post IDs. The source image never contains typography; typography is applied deterministically in the composition layer.
