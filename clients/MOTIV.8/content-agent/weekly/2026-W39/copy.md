# MOTIV.8 — Week 39 approval copy

## 01 — Reel: The 15-minute bridge

**On-screen copy**

1. Your plan changed. You have 15 minutes.
2. Do not rebuild the whole day.
3. Pick the next task that becomes easier after 15 minutes.
4. Open the document. Write the first three lines. Stop.
5. You made a bridge back to it.

**Caption**

When the original plan disappears, do not ask, “How do I catch up?” Ask, “What can I make easier for later?” Fifteen minutes can reopen a task without demanding a perfect recovery.

**CTA**

Save this for the next interrupted day.

## 03 — Reel: A 3-minute reset before you re-plan

**On-screen copy**

1. Before you re-plan: pause for 3 minutes.
2. Minute 1: name what changed.
3. Minute 2: cross out what no longer fits.
4. Minute 3: protect one next step.
5. Now make a smaller plan.

**Caption**

Replanning works better after you stop trying to preserve an old day. Name the change, release what no longer fits, then choose one thing to protect.

**CTA**

Try this before you open your calendar again.

## 04 — Static: Protect one thing before the week fills up

**Creative copy**

Before the week fills up, protect one thing that matters to you.

**Caption**

Choose one block for what keeps you well, connected, learning, or moving. Put it in the calendar before the week starts negotiating with you. It can be small. It just needs a place.

**CTA**

What are you protecting this week?
