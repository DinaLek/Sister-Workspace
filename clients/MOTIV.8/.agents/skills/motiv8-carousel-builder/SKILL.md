# MOTIV.8 Carousel Builder

## Purpose

Create practical, English-language Instagram carousels in the approved MOTIV.8 visual system. Each carousel must teach or enable one small action; motivational phrasing alone is not sufficient.

## Required input

Before production, define:

- topic/question and audience context
- learner takeaway
- practical action or experiment
- conceptual source, if any (use principles only; never reproduce copyrighted wording)
- slide count and copy per slide
- visual brief and caption/CTA

## Content structure

Prefer this sequence:

1. Clear English hook.
2. Problem or pattern recognition.
3. Short explanation that adds understanding.
4. Three to five steps, choices, or examples with enough detail to execute.
5. One worked example or a "when X, do Y" application.
6. One small action to try today.
7. Takeaway or gentle CTA.

Copy must be concise, natural English, specific, and useful. Remove generic affirmations, unsupported claims, and duplicate slides.

## Visual requirements

- 4:5 Instagram format.
- Use approved MOTIV.8 brand colors only; one accent color per frame.
- Light neutral backgrounds, generous whitespace, clean editorial composition.
- Use the approved Instrument Sans font files for all visible copy. Use the Regular face as the default and Medium only for deliberate word-level emphasis. Do not use Inter, Arial, synthetic bold, or another fallback in exported assets.
- The approved Drive reference folder is `1RboGd2NROnQxfJgcKaWay7qVd1zQQO8H`; inspect its approved files before creating a new visual system.
- Use a clean full-bleed background plane: no enclosing card, border, inset frame, default watermark, temporary brand name, or slide number.
- Cover display type must be visually dominant and deliberately line-broken. Treat 72px as a minimum working size at 1080x1350; reduce copy before reducing it below the approved headline scale.
- Match the approved composition: centre the active text block both horizontally and vertically. Use a light/regular display weight with generous line spacing; a thin forest-green underline is optional, not a default. Do not use top-left app-card positioning.
- Do not add a carousel title, tag, or other header at the top. Keep headline and any relevant supporting sentence in the middle of the page. Within one carousel, hold the display family, headline size, line-height, alignment, background, and accent constant; contrast comes from weight or one restrained accent word only, not a different font or arbitrary scale changes.
- Use the actual approved typeface files or a verified browser-loaded font. Never silently fall back to Arial or another system fallback for review assets.
- Before export, inspect the loaded font file's family and weight. A CSS weight declaration alone is not proof: reject a slide if its rendered type is visibly heavier or from a different Instrument Sans file than the approved reference.
- Lock one background and one accent token for every carousel; do not alternate background or accent colours between its slides. Use the approved warm beige `#F4F0E7` or the approved warm grey `#C9C6BE` exactly when a grey ground is chosen.
- Alongside warm beige, a warm soft-grey ground is approved. Teal (`#3C7F79`) and gold (`#D8A62B`) may be used only as restrained semantic emphasis: a word, underline, dot, route line, or small graphic element. They must not become a dominant background or compete with the headline.
- Use one small, subtle forward arrow only when it supports carousel navigation. Optional Instagram action icons may appear only on a final CTA slide, never as a default decoration.
- A supporting sentence is optional, never filler. Do not repeat a generic subtitle across slides: include it only when it adds a specific instruction, example, or clarification to that slide.
- Treat headline/support as one centred display block when both lines carry the idea. Do not shrink the second line into small body copy merely because it is technically a subtitle; rebreak the approved copy to fit at the established display scale.
- Do not add quotation marks, colons, periods, divider lines, or other punctuation/decorative separators unless they are semantically required by the approved copy. If a supporting line is absent, leave it absent rather than replacing it with a rule.
- Export files with semantic, human-readable names: `<content-piece>-<slide-number>-<short-slide-subject>.png`; never use `banner-02-1.png` or another anonymous sequence.
- Keep typography size and weight consistent across slides; opening and closing slides may vary. Reuse the system, not the slide: each slide needs materially different copy, hierarchy, and one visual device that clarifies that slide's specific point.
- Use at most one supporting graphic element per frame.
- Use natural editorial imagery: paths, nature, seasons, desks, notebooks, and everyday environments.
- People may appear from the side, from behind, or partially cropped; no direct eye contact.
- Never use generic AI portraits, victory poses, staged smiles, glossy stock-ad styling, charts, or overloaded checklists.
- Do not add labels such as `CAROUSEL`, `REEL`, or `TOOL`.

## QA gate

Fail the carousel until corrected if any item is false:

- The reader can identify a pattern or state.
- The reader learns a concrete insight.
- The reader has one clear action or experiment.
- The message is specific rather than generic.
- Every slide advances the idea.
- No adjacent slides repeat the same layout and message with only a word swap.
- Each slide makes a materially new contribution: a decision, a concrete step, an example, or a next action. Delete a redundant slide rather than filling it with a paraphrase of the hook.
- The carousel contains a worked example or an equally concrete application, not only principles.
- Copy is natural English and remains readable within safe margins.
- Typography, colors, whitespace, imagery, and slide consistency match the approved system.
- No prohibited imagery, labels, or unnecessary visual devices are present.
- No frame/card, watermark, page number, temporary brand name, or undersized type appears unless the approved reference explicitly calls for it.
- The approval package must be visually inspected against an approved PNG before upload; SVG source alone is not a visual QA pass.
- During revisions, change only the commented slide(s), replace those named Drive files in place, and preserve all approved slides and their identifiers.
- Any book inspiration is paraphrased and used as conceptual background only.
- When an approval-copy file is supplied, every exported slide must match its approved wording, punctuation, and examples exactly. Do not shorten, substitute, or add copy for layout convenience; flag a fit problem instead.
- Render and visually inspect every individual 1080x1350 slide against the approved reference before upload. Reject anything with an uncentred or clipped text block, visibly incorrect weight/fallback typeface, a border/frame, a temporary brand label, a page number, or inconsistent background/accent tokens.

## Deliverables

Produce a source/editable carousel, final 4:5 slide assets, a review preview, caption and CTA, a brief QA report, and a source/rights ledger for imagery.

## Weekly workflow contract

The weekly orchestrator must not send a carousel to the approval package until the content brief is complete and this QA gate passes. Failed items remain in working state for revision. Approved files belong in the weekly Drive folder's `final` and `previews` areas, with approval tracked separately.
